# Privacy Policy for TidyRoutine

**Last updated: September 21, 2026**

TidyRoutine is developed and published by Velzoroq.

## Data Collection

TidyRoutine does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

The app does not require a user account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud synchronization.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Information created in TidyRoutine, including:

- rooms,
- cleaning tasks,
- schedules and reminders,
- task completion history,
- app settings,

is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to any external server.

## Notifications

TidyRoutine may request permission to send local notifications so that it can remind you about scheduled tasks.

Notification scheduling and processing are performed locally on your device.

## Data Sharing

TidyRoutine does not share user data with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your TidyRoutine data remains stored locally on your device until it is deleted through available app functions, the application's data is cleared in your device settings, or the application is uninstalled.

Uninstalling TidyRoutine removes locally stored application data according to the behavior of your operating system.

## Children's Privacy

TidyRoutine is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not specifically designed for children.

## External Services

TidyRoutine may open external applications or websites when you choose options such as contacting support or viewing this Privacy Policy.

Those external services may process information according to their own privacy policies. TidyRoutine itself does not receive that information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TidyRoutine's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
